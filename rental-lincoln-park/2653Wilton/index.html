Overview

2653 N Wilton Avenue is a four-unit residential building in Lincoln Park that underwent a complete gut renovation in 2016, featuring three spacious 4-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartments and a 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom garden unit with updated systems, modern interiors, and in-unit laundry.

Floorplans

While detailed floor plans are not currently available, the building includes the following layouts:

  • Units 1, 2, and 3: Spacious 4BD/2BA apartments of approximately 1450 sq. ft., each with access to a rear porch connected to the back stairwell.
  • Unit G (Garden): A 1BD/1BA apartment of approximately 700 sq. ft..

Community

Located on a quiet residential street in Lincoln Park near Diversey and Broadway, 2653 N Wilton Avenue offers convenient access to neighborhood dining, parks, shopping, and public transit.

Lincoln Park

  • Grocery & Daily Essentials: Several grocery options are located nearby, including Trader Joe’s on Diversey Parkway and Target on Clark Street near Diversey. Larger stores such as Mariano’s (Broadway & Barry) and Costco on Clybourn Avenue are approximately a 10–12 minute drive.

  • Cafés & Dining: A wide range of cafés, restaurants, and neighborhood dining options can be found along Broadway, Clark Street, and Diversey Parkway, all within walking distance of the property.

  • Parks & Outdoor Space: The residence is a short walk from Lincoln Park, one of Chicago’s largest green spaces, offering access to the Lakefront Trail, Lake Michigan shoreline, and numerous recreational areas including running paths, bike trails, and open parkland.

  • Transportation: Public transit is easily accessible via the nearby Diversey CTA station (Brown and Purple Lines) and the Belmont CTA station (Red, Brown, and Purple Lines), along with several CTA bus routes along Broadway and Clark Street. Lake Shore Drive is also just minutes away, providing convenient access to downtown Chicago and other parts of the city.
