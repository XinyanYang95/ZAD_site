Overview

Located in the heart of Lincoln Park within the Arlington–Deming Historic District, 2420 N Geneva Terrace is a historic Chicago residence originally built in 1881 and fully renovated in 2024. The property combines classic architecture with modern finishes, updated infrastructure, and bright living spaces designed for comfortable urban living. Leasing began in 2025.
